ModernHUD (NeoForge 1.21.1 port)

This repository is an unofficial port of the ModernHud mod by xypp (originally for TaCZ on
1.20.1 Forge) to Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.

All textures, sound assets and the original design are the work of the original author and remain
under their copyright. The port only adapts the rendering pipeline, configuration system and the
integration layers to 1.21.1.

If you are the original author and want this repository taken down, please open an issue.

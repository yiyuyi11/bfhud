ModernHUD (NeoForge 1.21.1 port)

This repository is an unofficial port of ModernHud by xypp (originally for TaCZ on 1.20.1 Forge) to
Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.

The original mod is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0, and so is this port. Textures,
sound assets and the original design remain the work of the original author and are redistributed here
under the same license.

Changes made for this port: rendering pipeline, configuration system, compatibility layers
(TaCZ / Superb Warfare / LR Tactical / Modern Loadout / Xaero) and HUD-avoidance for Battlefield Ops.
